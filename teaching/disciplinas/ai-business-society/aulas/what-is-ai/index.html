Answer 2 — the colloquial definition

  • AI is whatever we have not got used to yetnobody wrote this one down, and it is the one in use everywhere
  • The AI effect — when it works, we stop calling it AIonlookers discount the behaviour — “that is not real intelligence”
  • The word marks what is new, not what the system does
  • So “we use AI” says nothing about a productthe label tracks novelty, not capability

Symbolic AI

A human states the knowledge. The machine applies it.

What AI Is, and How It Got Here · 12/40

Symbolic AI, defined

  • A human states the knowledge, explicitlyas facts and rules, written down in words
  • The machine stores it, searches it, applies itit adds nothing of its own
  • It knows nothing it was not givencompetence is capped by what the expert could put into words
  • The test — can you point at the line that decided?if you can, it is symbolic AI

How ELIZA works — the whole program

  • Find a keyword in what you typeda list of about forty, each with a priority
  • Match a pattern built around it“… i am …” · “… my …” · “… you are …”

  • Swap the pronouns“I am” → “you are” · “my” → “your”

Three limits of writing the rules down

  • The expert cannot say what they knowa senior underwriter knows a file is wrong and cannot give you the checklist
  • It works inside the rules and nowhere elsenot a worse answer outside them — no answer at all
  • Rules interact, so maintenance compoundsrule 900 silently breaks rule 12 · every rule is owned by someone forever

Why 2012 and not 1986

What AI Is, and How It Got Here · 31/40

From judging to producing

  • Symbolic and learning systems return a verdictis this spam · will this customer leave · is this position good
  • A generative one returns a thing that did not exista paragraph · an image · a contract clause · a line of code
What AI Is, and How It Got Here · 32/40

Four properties of generative systems

  • General-purposelearning from data meant a project per problem; generative AI means a subscription
  • No technical skill requiredwhich is why it arrived inside your organisation before anyone approved it
  • Non-deterministicask twice, get two answers
  • It fails fluentlyconfident, well-formed, wrong — and nothing in it notices
